Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. 100% Offline & Local Storage

SideNote Pro stores all your notes, drafts, and snippets solely inside Chrome's sandboxed local storage (chrome.storage.local). Zero data is ever sent to external cloud servers, third-party analytics, or AI training clusters.

2. No Account or Tracking Required

You do not need to create an account, log in with an email, or disclose any personal identity. Your notes remain entirely under your personal hardware ownership.

3. Chrome Permissions

SideNote Pro requests only storage (to persist your notes locally), activeTab (to identify the website domain), and contextMenus (to support right-click "Save selection to SideNote").
